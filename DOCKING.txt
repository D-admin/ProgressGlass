依附 / 扒边（0.4.4）

将角色拖到屏幕底部任务栏、左边或右边附近，松开后自动切换为半身扒边姿态。
按住角色拖回屏幕中间，即恢复全身；右键也可选择“依附 / 扒边 → 离开边框”。
右键“依附 / 扒边”可以直接选择底部、左边、右边，或关闭拖到边缘自动依附。
先点击想依附的应用窗口，再右键桌宠，选择“依附最近使用的窗口”及边框方向。
桌宠会跟随窗口移动和缩放；目标最小化、隐藏或关闭后回到屏幕边缘。
屏幕依附方向和相对位置会保存；重启后不恢复旧窗口句柄，改为屏幕依附。
依附时仍可眨眼、微笑，悬停查看任务气泡，点击固定气泡。

Edge docking (0.4.4)

Drag the pet near the bottom taskbar or either screen side and release to cling to that edge.
Drag back into the screen to restore the full-body pose, or use the Detach item in the docking submenu.
The right-click docking submenu also offers direct bottom/left/right placement and an auto-snap toggle.
To cling to a window, first activate that application, then right-click the pet and choose the recent-window submenu and an edge.
The pet follows the window as it moves or resizes. A minimized, hidden or closed target falls back to the screen edge.
Screen docking direction and relative position are saved. Window handles are not restored across restarts.
Blinking, smiling, hover details and click-to-pin remain available while docked.
